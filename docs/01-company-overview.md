# Company overview

TempLogic Systems is a small independent company building diagnostic and monitoring software for critical equipment: commercial HVAC, industrial and commercial refrigeration, and the computers and services that support them.

**Motto:** Better Diagnostics, Better Decisions, Better Lives.

## What we build

- **Brújula**: a Linux smart layer in three editions (Oricalco, Eudaimonia, Delta-T). [Overview](02-brujula.md)
- **Praecura**: predictive fault classification and anomaly detection for HVAC and refrigeration. [Overview](06-praecura.md)
- **Latenza**: a self-hosted heartbeat and uptime monitor. [Overview](07-latenza.md)

## How we work

- Engineering comes from field problems in HVAC and refrigeration service.
- Source code is private. Documentation is public.
- Claims carry a [status label](status-labels.md), a date and a scope.
- Our software observes equipment; it does not control it, and it is not a building management system.
- Our event sensors give notices, not alarms. None of our products is a listed fire, theft or life-safety system.

## Where we are

All products are IN DEVELOPMENT. No product is offered for sale or under contract, and none has been proven in production use. See [engineering milestones](08-engineering-milestones.md) for what has been verified and [roadmap](11-roadmap.md) for what is planned.
