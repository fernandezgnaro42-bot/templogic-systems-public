# Status labels

Every claim in this repository is classed with one of four labels.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **VERIFIED** | Checked by running it, on the date and in the scope stated beside the claim. |
| **IN DEVELOPMENT** | Being built. Parts exist; the whole is not finished or proven. |
| **PLANNED** | Intended. Not started, or not yet working. |
| **EXPERIMENTAL** | Tried in a laboratory or on partial data. Results are not evidence of field performance. |

Rules we follow:

- A test count is a statement about software verification in a controlled environment. It is not a statement about accuracy in the field.
- "VERIFIED" always comes with a date and a scope.
- What we have not proven is said plainly, next to what we have.
- Unknown means unsaid.
